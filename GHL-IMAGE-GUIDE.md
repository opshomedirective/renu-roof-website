# Image Guide: ReNu Roof West Texas

This guide lists every image on the site, its filename, and where it goes.

**How to use this guide in GoHighLevel**

> ⚠️ **The GitHub repo will be deleted.** Every file below must be uploaded to and served from the GHL Media Library. Never link to GitHub or Unsplash.

1. Upload every file in `/public/images` (also delivered in the `imagenes-para-ghl` folder) to the GHL Media Library. Do not rename them.
2. When this guide names a file, use the Media Library file with that exact name.
3. Use only the images on this list. Do not use GHL stock photos, AI-generated images or placeholders.

All photos are licensed stock (Unsplash License: free for commercial use, no attribution required). They were picked to look like Midland and the Permian Basin: pumpjacks, desert sunsets, brick ranch homes, hail and dust storms.

> **GoNano logos** were taken from GoNano's own certified-contractor site (gonanocertifiedcontractors.com). Only show them while ReNu Roof West Texas holds GoNano certification. Never recolor or stretch them.

> **Priority for the client:** get real photos of ReNu Roof applying the treatment and real before/after pairs of treated roofs. When they exist, use them on the Home hero, the Before/After slider and the Roof Rejuvenation page. Stock libraries have no credible photo of a spray treatment on asphalt shingles, so we did not fake one.

## 3. Service pages `/services/...`

Each service page uses three images:

- **Hero:** the full-screen background at the top of the page. It is also the image on that service's card everywhere else on the site.
- **Photo A:** the large photo in the intro section.
- **Photo B:** the small overlapping photo in the intro section. It is also the background of the CTA card at the bottom of the page.

| Service | URL | Hero | Photo A | Photo B |
